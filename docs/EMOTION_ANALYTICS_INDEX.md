## 💡 Key Concepts

### Emotion Confidence
- **Before**: Usually 0.3 (heuristics fallback)
- **After**: Varies 0.1-0.95 based on detection quality
- **Why**: Helps identify reliable vs uncertain detections

### Emotion Intensity Levels
- **Low** (0.0-0.33): Uncertain, poor conditions
- **Medium** (0.33-0.66): Reasonable, normal conditions  
- **High** (0.66-1.0): Confident, clear expression

### Detection Method Tracking
- **DeepFace**: Most reliable, heavier computation
- **Keras**: Lighter, good accuracy in controlled conditions
- **Heuristics**: Very fast, least reliable (fallback)

### Face Quality Score
- Based on: size, brightness, contrast
- Higher score = better detection conditions
- Used to weight confidence scores
